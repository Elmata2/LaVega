## Who I am

I am Alexander Steunenberg, founder of LaVega. I am a student in the CEMS master's programme at Rotterdam School of Management (RSM). My thesis studies how investors' non-financial support affects startup growth.

## Why I build LaVega

Middle-class households often have several income streams and accounts at more than one bank, sometimes with investments on top. A clear overview is missing, and many people do not know where to start. Companies have a CFO; households usually do not. LaVega is my attempt to make that overview available.

## What LaVega is and is not

LaVega gives information and explanation. LaVega is not a financial adviser and has no AFM licence (the AFM is the Dutch financial regulator). The pages on this site describe how things work and which questions you can ask yourself; they do not say which product, provider or amount suits you. That decision stays with you.

Every page shows who wrote it, when it was last updated and which sources were used. See a mistake or something unclear? Let us know via lavega.dev.
