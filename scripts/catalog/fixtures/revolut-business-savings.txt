## FAQs

*   Savings is available to all businesses, across all our plans. Interest rates vary depending on your selected plan.

[Learn more about Savings.](https://help.revolut.com/business/help/managing-my-business/savings/?geo-redirect=true) 
*   You can deposit up to €50,000,000 in Savings. This limit applies to the money you put into your Savings account, not the interest you earn. You can still earn interest on your balance once you've reached your Savings limit.

[Learn more about Savings.](https://help.revolut.com/business/help/managing-my-business/savings/?geo-redirect=true) 
*   Interest is calculated and paid to your Savings account daily. Deposits are processed and begin earning interest the following day. Interest is based on the cleared account balance and the applicable daily interest rate, with payments made every day.

The interest rate depends on the type of plan you have. Paid plan subscription fees and T&Cs apply. Interest offered is subject to change, and any interest earned is liable to the applicable taxes.

[Learn more about Savings.](https://help.revolut.com/business/help/managing-my-business/savings/?geo-redirect=true) 
*   Your savings are protected by the Lithuanian Deposit and Investment Insurance Scheme for up to €100,000, and you can withdraw your assets securely using biometric authentication. 
*   
Before you open or choose a business savings account, you should consider the following factors:

    *   **Interest rates on offer** — how much could you earn with your savings?
    *   **How your money's protected** — does the savings account offer deposit protection?
    *   **Savings limits** — how much money are you looking to leave in your savings account, and will this fit within the limits of the account you're considering?
    *   **Access to your savings**— are your savings locked up for a set period of time? Are there any fees or penalties for early withdrawal? If you need access to your savings regularly, choose an account like Revolut Business Savings, which lets you withdraw anytime with no fees.
    *   **When interest is paid out** — consider how frequently interest is paid into your savings account. Revolut Business pays interest daily to maximise the value of your savings.

*   You can add or withdraw money whenever you need to, with zero restrictions. And you can set controls for who else gets access, too.
